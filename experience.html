Blake Hisey

Sports/News Reporter | Play-By-Play | Creative Services | Social Media

KOMU-8

NBC Affiliate - Columbia, Missouri

I began working at KOMU 8, Columbia, Missouri's NBC affiliate, during my junior year as an associate producer while also gaining experience as a digital producer. In this role, I developed skills in identifying newsworthy stories, writing for both television and the web, creating and presenting information through graphics, and distributing digital content to enhance website traffic.

At the beginning of senior year, I transitioned to news reporting, where I pitched, produced, wrote, and edited TV packages on tight deadlines. I presented my stories live as on-air talent both in-studio and in the field to approximately 117,000 households in mid-Missouri.

At the beginning of my final semester on campus, I transitioned once more to sports reporting for KOMU 8 Sports to compile all the skills I have gathered in a sports-specific environment.

WDAF Fox 4 KC

Nexstar Media Group - Kansas City, Missouri

After my junior year, I expanded my skill set by working as a creative services intern for Fox 4 Kansas City, owned by Nexstar Media Group. In this role, I helped brainstorm, plan, and execute promotional projects, including Fox 4's Red Friday "Road To Red-Emption."

I attended commercial shoots that strengthened my understanding of camera shooting and lighting, while also strengthening my skills in Photoshop, Premiere Pro, and After Effects. In addition, I assisted the station's lifestyle show, Great Day KC, through floor directing and web writing, giving me a behind-the-scenes look at production and on-air workflows.

Sports Radio 810 WHB

Union Broadcasting, Inc. - Overland Park, KS

After my sophomore year, I worked as an intern with Sports Radio 810 WHB and ESPN Kansas City 1510 AM, stations owned by Union Broadcasting, Inc. During my time there, I learned how on-air hosts build their shows using guests, statistics, and audience-engaging segments, while also gaining hands-on production experience.

I had opportunities as an on-air guest on 810 AM, the largest sports radio station in the United States by signal area. At the conclusion of the internship, my fellow interns and I created, produced, and hosted an original show for ESPN 1510 AM, which aired live for a total of four hours over two evenings.

LSR7 School District

Sports Director: Tiger Broadcast - Lee's Summit, Missouri

Throughout my time as the Sports Director for TBTV 8 Sports, I was given numerous opportunities to develop and showcase my on-air voice. Over three years, I provided play-by-play and color commentary for dozens of Lee's Summit High School's athletic events, including football, basketball, soccer, volleyball, and wrestling.

Our team worked through the COVID-19 pandemic, when limited in-person attendance increased the demand for live-streamed games. Through problem-solving and innovation, such as implementing a live sideline reporter, we grew TBTV 8's existing YouTube subscriber count by over 400% while offering the public free access to local sports coverage in a safe way.

During my junior year, I became the public address announcer for Lee's Summit High School's baseball team at its former home, Legacy Park. As a senior, I continued in that role after the program moved to its new baseball and softball complex, announcing not only home games but also high-stakes district tournaments and "The Game," an alumni-only matchup that served as the inaugural event at the new facility. In addition, I served as the emcee for Lee's Summit High School's National Letter of Intent signing ceremonies, all of which were streamed live.

For a list of livestream awards given to my team and I, visit the Awards section of my resume.
