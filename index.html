About Me

After discovering my passion for broadcast journalism in high school, I attended the University of Missouri School of Journalism in Columbia, Missouri to build a well-rounded skill set in the field. I began developing my newsroom foundation during my junior year as an associate and digital producer at KOMU 8, before transitioning into news and sports reporting my senior year.

Working as a student journalist sharpened my ability to pitch, produce, write, and edit packages for TV on tight deadlines. I also gained experience shooting video and writing web stories using AP style, all of which strengthened my storytelling and gave me hands-on, real world newsroom experience.

Outside of Columbia, I diversified my experience through summer internships in Kansas City. After my sophomore year, I interned with Sports Radio 810 WHB and ESPN Kansas City 1510 AM, where I learned how hosts build their shows with guests, statistics, and audience-engaging segments. I also gained production and social media experience, had various opportunities to guest on 810 AM, and concluded the internship by helping create, produce, and host an original show for ESPN 1510 AM with my fellow interns.

Following my junior year, I worked as a creative services intern at Fox 4 KC, owned by Nexstar Media Group. In this role, I helped plan and execute promotional projects such as Fox 4's Red Friday "Road To Red-Emption," attended commercial shoots that furthered my camera and lighting knowledge, and strengthened my skills in Photoshop, Premiere Pro, and After Effects. I also assisted the station's lifestyle show, "Great Day KC," through floor directing and web writing.

Outside of work and school, I enjoy playing soccer, watching sports, spending time outdoors, and relaxing with family and friends.
